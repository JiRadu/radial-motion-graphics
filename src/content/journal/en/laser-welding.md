On a metal piece, the weld is where the quality of the work shows most. A wide, distorted or discoloured seam spoils an otherwise beautiful part. That's why we invested in laser welding.

## What laser welding is

Laser welding uses a concentrated beam of light that melts the material over a very narrow zone. The result is a fine, strong joint with a much smaller heat-affected zone than conventional welding.

## What you gain

- **Clean, narrow seams** that are easier to finish.
- **Minimal distortion**, because the part heats up less — key on thin sheet.
- **Less discolouration** on stainless.
- **Repeatability** across batches.

## Where we use it

Laser welding makes the difference on steel, stainless and aluminium, especially on visible parts: furniture, table bases, decorative pieces, housings. For large structures we use the process suited to the material thickness.
