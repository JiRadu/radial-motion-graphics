Mirror polish is the most demanding finish we do. It forgives nothing: every scratch, every unfinished weld, every dip shows up instantly in the reflection. That's exactly why it looks so good when it's done right.

## What "mirror finish" means

A mirror finish is stainless steel polished until it reflects an image clearly, like a mirror. It takes several sanding stages, from coarse to very fine grit, followed by polishing with special compounds.

## Why it takes time

- **The weld has to disappear.** Before polishing, every joint is ground flush with the surrounding material.
- **Each stage removes the marks of the last.** Skip a grit and the scratches stay visible in the reflection.
- **Corners and inside radii** are worked by hand, patiently.

## Where it's worth it

Mirror polish makes the difference on pieces meant to catch the eye: bar stools and tables for restaurants, decorative objects, signage letters and panels. In a warmly lit space, polished stainless picks up the colours around it and becomes part of the atmosphere.

## Care

Polished stainless is cleaned with water and a soft cloth, never abrasive sponges. For fingerprints, a dedicated stainless cleaner is enough.
