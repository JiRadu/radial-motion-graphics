One of the first questions we ask on a quote request is: "Which material do you prefer?" Many clients answer "not sure yet" — and that's perfectly fine. Here's how we think about it.

## At a glance

| Material | Good for | Finishes | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Carbon steel** | structures, halls, frames, painted furniture | painted, anti-corrosion coating | strong and economical; needs protection |
| **Stainless steel** | hospitality furniture, decorative pieces, railings | mirror polish, brushed | doesn't rust, hygienic, premium look |
| **Aluminium** | light parts, furniture bases, housings | brushed, painted | light, doesn't rust, needs careful welding |
| **Corten steel** | firepits, outdoor pieces, landscaping | natural patina | forms its own protective layer |

## Carbon steel

The base material for steel structures: halls, warehouses, PV mounting structures, canopies. It's strong and easy to work, but needs an anti-corrosion coating or paint.

## Stainless steel

Stainless is the choice for pieces that must look flawless and be easy to clean. It takes the finest finishes — brushed or mirror polish — and doesn't rust.

## Aluminium

Aluminium is light and doesn't rust. We use it for table bases, furniture parts and components where weight matters. Laser welding lets us make fine joints with minimal distortion.

## Corten steel

Corten forms a stable, rust-coloured patina that protects the material. It needs no paint and has a warm, natural character — ideal for firepits and outdoor pieces.

**Not sure which to choose?** Tell us where the piece will live and how it will be used. We'll recommend the right material.
