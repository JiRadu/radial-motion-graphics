The more we know about your project, the more accurate the quote — and the faster we get to the finished piece. Here's what helps in an email or on the phone.

## The short list

1. **Name, company (if any), phone and email.**
2. **What you'd like made:** furniture, decorative piece, structure, railing, gate, something else.
3. **Where it will be used:** indoors or outdoors; home, restaurant, office, hall.
4. **Rough dimensions and quantity.**
5. **Preferred material:** stainless, aluminium, steel, corten — or "not sure yet".
6. **Desired finish:** mirror polish, brushed, painted (which colour) — or "not sure yet".
7. **Sketches, inspiration photos or drawings** — attach them.
8. **Transport and installation:** needed? Where?
9. **Target date.**
10. **Indicative budget** (optional, but it helps us suggest the right option).

## Have technical files?

For sheet-metal parts, send **DXF**. For 3D parts, send **STEP**. If you don't use CAD, a hand-drawn dimensioned sketch is a great start.
