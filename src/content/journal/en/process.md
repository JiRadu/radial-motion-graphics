Every job that leaves our workshop goes through the same four stages. We've refined them over more than 25 years, and our advantage is simple: they all happen in one place.

## 01 — Design

We start from what you have: an idea, an inspiration photo, a paper sketch or a DXF/STEP file. Together we agree dimensions, material and finish, then make the fabrication drawing. If something can be simplified without changing the result, we'll say so.

## 02 — Fabrication

This is where the technology comes in:

- **CNC laser cutting** for precise contours in sheet, tube and profiles;
- **plasma cutting** for heavy plate;
- **CNC press-brake bending** for exact, repeatable angles;
- **laser welding** for clean joints with minimal distortion.

## 03 — Finishing

The finish is what you see and touch. We grind the joints, then apply the chosen finish: mirror polish, brushed or painted. Every piece is checked before it leaves the workshop.

## 04 — Delivery & installation

We pack the pieces so they arrive intact. When needed, we install on site — whether it's a railing in a home or the frame of an industrial hall.

> The whole process in one place means fewer middlemen, direct communication and one party responsible for the result.
